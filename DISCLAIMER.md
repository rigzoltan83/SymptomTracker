## English

SymptomTracker is intended solely as a personal diary, data recording
and informational analysis tool.

It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, prevent,
monitor, predict, prognose, treat or alleviate any disease or medical
condition.

Information, statistical associations, scores, risk indicators or
other results produced by the software must not be interpreted as a
medical diagnosis.

An association found between a food, ingredient, medication or other
recorded event and a symptom does not establish causation.

The software must not be used as a substitute for advice, diagnosis
or treatment provided by a qualified healthcare professional.

they enter into or obtain from the software.
